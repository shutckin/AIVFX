## Notes

- `shot-10-final-card.png` was built locally to keep the text exact.
- `alternate-shot-01-monitor-wipe.png` is kept as a spare first-shot option.
- Best shots for immediate video animation: 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10.
- Shots 04 and 05 work as rhythm inserts, but can be regenerated later if the reel needs a more premium middle section.

## Recommended Animation Direction

- Shot 01: slow wipe motion, water/dust revealing the mark.
- Shot 02: tiny red pulse along laser engraving.
- Shot 03: smoke swirls inward, mark holds for half a second.
- Shot 04: subtle coffee ripple.
- Shot 05: slight handheld drift, no major skin deformation.
- Shot 06: film strip moves a few millimeters across light table.
- Shot 07: stamp lifts, wax catches light.
- Shot 08: fabric shifts slightly, thread glints.
- Shot 09: spray can finishes the eye area, paint drips down.
- Shot 10: static logo card with a small red glow pulse.
