# AIVFX Robot Teaser Keyframes — Round 06 Lifestyle

Date: 2026-05-28

## Direction

This round moves beyond production props into everyday lifestyle scenes. The AIVFX robot appears as a real-world mark people might casually carry, notice, draw, or pass by:

- notebook doodle in a cafe
- tote bag at a crosswalk
- phone case in the city
- laptop sticker in a coworking cafe
- street pole sticker
- backpack pin during commute
- night creator desk
- hoodie sleeve tag
- crossbody bag patch
- cafe/studio entrance poster

The goal is a truer lifestyle feel: people, motion, daylight, city texture, cafe warmth, and small-to-medium brand moments.
